# LihatDulu V5.1 identity system

LihatDulu helps people choose pre-owned furniture by looking closely before choosing. The identity treats inspection as an act of care: a clear frame, a tactile object, and enough evidence to make a considered decision.

## Mark rationale

The selected mark combines an open aperture and a furniture frame. The negative space reads as a deliberate look, while the lower horizontal bar anchors the mark like a cabinet or shelf. It remains recognisable at 16px because it uses one uninterrupted silhouette and two unequal openings rather than detail lines.

## Colour roles

The palette uses bone as the default field and charcoal for readable copy. Moss carries primary action and the trust of an inspection surface. Catalogue yellow marks active discovery and saved or selected states. Sand and clay are supporting material references, not decorative gradients. Capture grey belongs to the immersive scene and inherits the same typography and focus treatment.

## Type

Plus Jakarta Sans is the interface workhorse and is bundled locally. Georgia is reserved for occasional editorial display such as the role gateway or brand story, where a measured serif gives furniture a human register without making the product feel luxurious or distant.

## Composition

Use a 12-column desktop grid with 24px gutters and a 40px outer margin. On small screens use a 4-column grid with 16px gutters and 20px outer margin. Keep the mark aligned to the same optical left edge as catalogue headings. Let object photography carry visual weight; controls should be quiet, rectangular, and easy to read.

## Control language

Primary actions use moss with bone text. Secondary actions use a 1px charcoal or moss outline. Catalogue yellow is a state marker and never carries long body text. Focus uses the blue token with a 3px ring and 4px offset. Capture uses large 48px controls and one reachable Next action.

## Product evidence

Each piece uses one authoritative record across catalogue, product, seller and checkout. A condition note names what the viewer can see. Dimension facts use centimetres and retain uncertainty where a real room fit cannot be guaranteed. A prepared example remains clearly local and illustrative.

## Asset usage

Logo sources live in `app/public/brand/`. Use `logo-lockup.svg` on light backgrounds, `logo-lockup-reverse.svg` on moss or charcoal, `logo-mark.svg` for favicon and compact mobile header, and `favicon.svg` for browser chrome. Do not recreate the mark using text.
