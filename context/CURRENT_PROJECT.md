## Technology boundaries

The website demonstrates authored mesh inspection, articulation, disclosed wear, prepared capture, geometric example placement and review-only commerce. It does not supply real camera capture, Gaussian-splat reconstruction, live inventory, real seller verification, messaging, shipping quotes or payments.

The redesign remains a prepared product concept. The user selected predetermined camera/search examples and does not require judges to provide personal information or setup.
