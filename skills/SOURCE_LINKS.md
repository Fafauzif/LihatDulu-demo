Use native available artifact skills instead where appropriate. Third-party skill licences and environmental assumptions apply. Do not assume that an external skill's advertised libraries are installed.

The Web Vitals goals in the mobile specification come from Google/Chrome guidance. Field compliance requires the 75th percentile of real page loads, segmented appropriately. Proposed transfer, frame-rate and asset budgets are team planning targets.

The Gaussian-splatting paper describes a rendering/reconstruction research method. It does not verify this product's pipeline, dimensional accuracy or automated articulation.
