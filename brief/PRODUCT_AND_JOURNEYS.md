# Product journeys and role navigation

## Role gateway

The root remains a buyer/seller choice. Rework its composition within the new identity, with two understandable actions and matching furniture imagery. On phones, both choices must be discoverable without oversized marketing content pushing one far down the page.

Use a distinct way to return to role selection. Normal desktop navigation exposes buying and selling. Mobile navigation provides an obvious role switch, with text labels rather than unexplained icons. Changing roles changes the view, not the stored favourites, collections, cart or capture selections.

Account creation is not required to inspect any prepared path.

## Buyer demonstration

A judge can:
1. Choose Buyer.
2. Browse four available examples and two coming soon.
3. Search or filter to a meaningful result.
4. Open a selected item.
5. Rotate its actual model, inspect dimensions and disclosed condition, or use its image fallback.
6. Visit its sample seller storefront or save the item to a collection.
7. Add the one-off item to a cart.
8. Review pickup/delivery and totals.
9. Return to the same item or switch to Seller.

Use Arca as one coherent additional-product journey. Sora remains the flagship for articulation, wear and prepared room-clearance demonstration.

Keep a complete buyer journey short enough to understand without reading a manual. Do not force a judge through every optional action.

## Seller demonstration

A judge can:
1. Choose Seller.
2. See a prepared seller workspace containing the Sora example and meaningful listing status.
3. Tap the prominent Sora capture action.
4. Explore the viewport-filling prepared capture screen.
5. Select example frames with the shutter or move between areas.
6. Review matching photos, details and disclosed wear.
7. Preview the corresponding buyer-facing Sora listing.
8. Return to the workspace or switch to Buyer.

The workspace is populated, not an empty onboarding form. Useful seller tools include editable example listing details, condition disclosure, listing preview and availability controls for the local example. Do not imply a real publication service. Clearly distinguish local preview/status changes from actual marketplace publication.

Keep edits in a local draft layer. Description, example price and local availability can be edited, but judges must not silently change the authoritative model dimensions, geometry or evidenced wear. Additional unverified seller notes remain identified as notes. Revalidate and refresh cart totals when an example price or availability changes. Cancel/undo should restore the prior draft without overwriting the asset manifest.

## Navigation and state

- Use real links for destinations and buttons for actions.
- Preserve meaningful query, filter and tab state in URLs.
- Direct routes and refreshes work through the existing SPA rewrite.
- Back navigation returns to a sensible prior view, including catalogue filters.
- Each product has an independent gallery and viewer session.
- Entering capture does not erase the buyer cart or saved room positions.
- Closing capture restores normal site navigation and scroll position.
- Provide a reset of prepared example state with undo where appropriate, without clearing unrelated browser data.
- Empty and unavailable states provide useful recovery destinations.

## Motion and interaction

Motion should explain state changes, selection, camera movement and mechanical opening. Avoid cinematic scroll hijacking, autoplay marketing effects and continuous decorative motion. Reduced motion affects engine movement as well as CSS.

On product pages, normal vertical scrolling must remain usable. Rotation, zoom and moving furniture have tap/button alternatives. Capture is an explicit immersive mode with a reachable exit.
