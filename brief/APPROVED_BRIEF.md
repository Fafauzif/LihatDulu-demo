# Approved brief

## Product and audience

LihatDulu is a curated marketplace concept for pre-owned furniture. Its distinctive proposition is inspecting an individual piece, understanding disclosed wear and assessing its dimensions before choosing it.

The primary presentation audience is competition judges. The interface must also make sense as a normal shopping and selling product. Use natural Indonesian customer copy.

The user already likes the prototype's overall craft but wants a more convincing marketplace ecosystem and an especially strong mobile experience. The user subsequently authorized full brand reinvention.

## Confirmed scope

| Decision | Approved treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Root entrance | Preserve the buyer/seller role chooser |
| Role switching | Easy, visible and available throughout normal journeys |
| Buyer landing | Functional marketplace |
| Seller landing | Populated prepared workspace with prominent Sora capture action |
| Available examples | Sora cabinet, Arca chair, Batu table, Rimba sideboard |
| Coming soon | Lino sofa and Nara lounge |
| 3D | Distinct rendered models and pages for all four available examples |
| Capture | Sora only, prepared example workflow |
| Mobile capture | Viewport-filling grey scene, large Sora, minimal controls, progress and Next |
| Photo search | Predetermined object, prepared similarity results, separate size filters |
| Permissions | No real camera, microphone, personal upload or required account |
| Brand | New original vector logo and complete identity direction |
| Brand artifacts | Editable PPTX and matching exported PDF |
| Mobile 3D | Adaptive automatic loading or tap-to-load under constrained conditions |
| Final application export | Updated source plus fresh prebuilt dist/, ready for Vercel |

All four available listings are authored demonstration examples, not proof of live inventory. Keep that distinction contextual and clear without placing a large prototype banner across every screen.

## Non-negotiable product coherence

- A product card opens that product.
- Coming-soon cards open their own honest preview, not an unrelated Sora redirect.
- Catalogue photos, GLBs, dimensions and condition notes describe the same piece.
- Single-item inventory and cart behaviour agree.
- Role switching preserves valid state without carrying a different item's camera or conditions into the new product.
- No fake reconstruction progress, automatic damage grading, verified seller badges, sales counts, testimonials or payment success.
- A size constraint match is not a guarantee that furniture fits a real room.
- The full mobile experience remains navigable when 3D is unavailable.

## Priority order

1. Complete, truthful buyer and seller journeys.
2. Responsive mobile usability and weaker-device performance.
3. Consistent product assets and functioning inspection.
4. Distinctive brand and polished marketplace design.
5. Presentation artifacts and reproducibility.
6. Optional enhancements that do not weaken the core.

Do not expand into fifty listings, live computer vision, live payments, a backend marketplace, a new film or unrelated social features. A prepared offer interaction is optional only after the core passes.
