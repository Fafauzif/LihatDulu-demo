# Original brand and professional guidelines

## Creative brief

Full identity reinvention is authorized. Keep the name LihatDulu and the purpose of looking closely before choosing furniture. The previous palette and logo are historical starting material, not locked requirements.

Create a distinctive, approachable furniture-marketplace identity suited to Indonesian mobile shoppers and a competition presentation. Aim for confidence, clarity and recognizability without generic technology imagery or a luxury-only tone.

Etsy references inform shopping interfaces. The supplied brand PDF informs the structure of a usable corporate guideline. Do not reproduce either company's identity.

## Original vector logo

Develop several materially different original vector concepts internally, evaluate them at practical sizes and refine one direction. Build the final symbol and wordmark through SVG/code or another editable vector construction.

Brand marks are an explicit exception to upstream bans on hand-authored SVG icons. Use a consistent existing icon family for ordinary interface glyphs if useful.

Test:
- Symbol alone and symbol with wordmark.
- Small favicon and mobile-header use.
- Light and dark backgrounds.
- Monochrome and reversed treatment.
- Transparent background.
- Clear silhouette, spacing and letterform legibility.

Do not claim trademark clearance or professional market validation. Show rationale grounded in visible construction and product purpose.

Deliver original source SVGs, practical PNG exports, favicon variants and the construction code or editable source. Use licensed typography and retain notices.

## One design system

Define reusable tokens for palette, typography, spacing, radius, borders, surfaces, shadows, status colours, focus and interaction. Document their roles and apply them to website and brand guide.

The neutral grey capture environment is an intentional task-specific surface. It must still use the same control language, typography and accessibility standards.

Avoid arbitrary visual changes between buyer, seller and capture. Use restrained motion and a consistent icon treatment. The identity must survive the lightweight renderer.

## Brand-guidelines artifact

Create an editable widescreen PPTX, approximately 18-22 purposeful slides. Adjust count for coherent coverage rather than filling a quota.

Cover:
1. Brand purpose and identity overview.
2. Selected logo and construction rationale.
3. Symbol, wordmark and lockup variants.
4. Clear space and minimum-size rules with measurable diagrams.
5. Monochrome, reversed and background treatments.
6. Incorrect usage with actual examples.
7. Colour palette and functional roles.
8. Typography hierarchy and licensing.
9. Layout, grid and spacing principles.
10. Product imagery and condition-evidence treatment.
11. Iconography, controls and interaction states.
12. Marketplace card, product page and seller-workspace applications.
13. Mobile navigation and immersive capture application.
14. Motion, reduced motion and adaptive-quality principles.
15. Co-branding or partner treatment as an example, without fabricated endorsements.
16. Practical asset index and concise implementation guidance.

Use the supplied reference PDF's specification-and-application structure. Exclude its brand-specific symbolism, partner claims and drafting notes.

Build editable text, shapes, tables and construction diagrams. Use screenshots of the final implemented site for applications where useful. A slide-sized screenshot is not an editable brand guide.

Export the PDF from the completed PPTX. Compare slide/page counts and rendered layouts. Check every page for cropping, overflow, illegible type, missing fonts or inconsistent colours. Update the guide after final website changes.

## Public copy

Use natural Indonesian and concrete customer language. Keep implementation commentary, prompt references and review ratings outside the public assets. Retain relevant and truthful explanations of prepared experiences or proposed technical capabilities.
