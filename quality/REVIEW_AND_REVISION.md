# Demanding review and revision

Apply a demanding, evidence-led review-and-revision process to the authorized LihatDulu redesign. Evaluate the actual implementation and exported artifacts, not the ambition of the plan.

Before implementation, convert the approved brief into an explicit acceptance checklist. Cover the buyer/seller gateway, role switching, marketplace journeys, individual product identities, prepared capture, prepared visual search, mobile layouts, original logo, brand guidelines, source files and deployment export.

Respect confirmed decisions. Ask only about unresolved choices that materially affect scope, interpretation or implementation. Resolve routine design and engineering choices independently.

Produce a complete first implementation before final evaluation. Do not substitute disconnected mockups, decorative controls or descriptions of intended behaviour for working journeys.

Review the result as a demanding evaluator. When independent agents are available, use a critic who examines the requirements and actual outputs. Give the critic sufficient evidence to challenge the implementation. Otherwise perform a distinct review pass. Claim independent review only when it occurred.

Score each applicable dimension separately:
- Requirement fulfillment and scope discipline.
- Brand originality, logo construction and visual coherence.
- Marketplace navigation and commercial consistency.
- Agreement between models, photographs, dimensions and condition information.
- Mobile usability, especially inspection and full-screen capture.
- Functional correctness, accessibility, performance and recovery states.
- Brand-guidelines quality, editability and agreement with the website.
- Reproducibility and completeness of the final export.

Use this calibration strictly:
- 5: substantial defects or missing work.
- 7: usable in parts, with clear weaknesses.
- 8: acceptable professional work, but below this project's intended standard.
- 8.5: great work, with only minor, bounded weaknesses.
- 9: excellent, cohesive work supported by broad verification.
- 9.5: amazing work requiring substantial evidence of exceptional execution across realistic conditions.
- 10: a defensible breakthrough across every applicable dimension, supported by extraordinary evidence. A polished prototype or passing test suite alone cannot justify this score.

Justify every score with observable strengths, defects and verification limits. Do not average away a weak dimension. Unmet explicit requirements and known critical errors fail the review regardless of the average score.

Verify complete user journeys, direct links, refreshes, back navigation, role switching, saved state, search and filter results, cart arithmetic and unavailable-item behaviour.

Inspect model movement and geometry where applicable. Confirm that every listing uses its own matching assets and dimensions. Test model-loading failures, rapid scene switching and independence of saved state between products.

Mobile performance is a release gate. Inspect cold/warm loads, recorded throttling conditions, adaptive profiles, frame behaviour, offscreen/idle work and repeated renderer lifecycles. Mobile screenshots alone cannot establish weaker-device performance. Distinguish hardware tests, browser emulation and software rendering. Do not invent measured budgets or certification.

Inspect mobile and desktop screenshots, touch behaviour, keyboard operation, focus visibility, reduced motion and safe-area handling.

Render and inspect the brand-guidelines PPTX and PDF. Check for clipping, overlap, missing assets, inconsistent tokens and unintended differences between formats. Confirm that the PPTX contains editable content.

Rank findings by impact. Fix critical errors and unmet requirements first, then substantive weaknesses, then polish. Recheck affected behaviour for regressions.

Perform up to four review-and-revision rounds. Aim for excellent work. Finish only when every applicable dimension reaches at least 8.5, no critical or major issue remains, and further revisions lack a concrete material benefit.

Never increase scores merely to satisfy the threshold. If the round limit or an external constraint prevents meeting the standard, deliver the strongest verified result and disclose the remaining limitation. A missing physical-device test may be disclosed as a bounded gap, but cannot become a false hardware-performance claim.

Keep scores, critique exchanges and review transcripts outside the public website and brand guidelines. Keep the final handoff brief and factual.
