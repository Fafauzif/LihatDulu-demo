# Finished deliverable: language and design discipline

Create a finished, distinctive LihatDulu marketplace and brand system for competition judges and prospective users.

Use natural Indonesian for the customer interface. Write specific, concise copy that helps users discover furniture, understand its condition, inspect it and navigate the transaction concept. Avoid generic marketing filler, inflated promises, repetitive summaries, em dashes and semicolons.

Preserve the approved buyer/seller entrance and make switching roles straightforward. Keep ordinary customer journeys free of unnecessary setup, account requirements and personal-data entry.

Use Etsy references to inform shopping behaviour, hierarchy and mobile interaction. Develop an original LihatDulu identity rather than reproducing Etsy's branding or inserting reference screenshots into the website.

Create the logo as an original vector asset through code. Ensure that its silhouette and wordmark remain legible at small sizes, in monochrome and on light and dark backgrounds. Deliver reproducible source and practical exports.

Build one coherent design system covering typography, colours, spacing, surfaces, icons, controls and interaction states. Apply it consistently to the website and brand-guidelines presentation.

Use restrained, purposeful composition. Marketplace product grids, filter sheets and status labels should serve shopping tasks. Avoid decoration added solely to fill space, excessive borders, arbitrary gradients and generic feature-card sections.

Make every available product internally consistent. Its model, product photographs, measurements, category, materials and disclosed condition must describe the same piece. Keep coming-soon products clearly distinguished and unavailable for purchase.

Make prepared experiences understandable. Visual search must show the predetermined reference object and its prepared matches. Size filtering must use catalogue dimensions independently of the photograph. Capture must show example viewpoints and frame selection without implying that reconstruction has occurred.

Preserve necessary uncertainty and scope information. Use concise, contextual labels for example content and proposed services. Do not invent reviews, verified sellers, transactions, research, technical capabilities, guarantees or human authorship.

Keep assistant commentary, drafting notes, review scores, prompt references and assistant-directed handoff/build instructions out of the public interface and brand guidelines. Legitimate brand-usage rules, asset specifications and design-system implementation guidance belong in the brand guide. Technical explanations may describe relevant product capabilities accurately. Removing generic writing does not require hiding material technical limits or omitting truthful scope labels.

Produce an editable, professionally composed brand-guidelines PPTX. Include logo construction and variants, clear space, minimum sizes, incorrect usage, colour roles, typography, imagery, interface components and actual applications. Export the PDF from the finished presentation and inspect both formats.

Use genuine, traceable evidence for external factual claims. Distinguish planning assumptions from verified findings. Include appropriate references where substantive external evidence is used. Identify supplied references by meaningful titles in audience-facing material. Use real bibliographic details and APA 7 references where appropriate to the content.

Before delivery, remove drafting residue and inspect the complete output for consistency, usability and layout defects. Confirm that functional controls work and that source files reproduce the delivered assets.

Audience-facing cleanup rules apply to the website, brand guidelines and public copy. Internal implementation documentation should retain the filenames, technical instructions and asset provenance needed to maintain the project.

Do not promise that authorship can be inferred from style or that text is undetectable as AI-generated. Judge the specificity, clarity, originality and accuracy of the work.
